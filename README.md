# 📦 Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis

**Data Analyst Project 3 | Mentor: Tarun Kumar**

An end-to-end **Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis** project focused on understanding inventory levels, product sales, supplier performance, delivery delays, and profitability using a dataset of **15,000 records**.

The project covers the complete data analytics workflow — from **data cleaning and analysis to business insights and Power BI dashboard development**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze supply chain and inventory data to identify:

* Inventory shortages and overstock situations
* Fast-moving and slow-moving products
* Supplier delivery performance
* Delivery delays
* Product profitability
* The relationship between inventory, delivery performance, and sales

The final goal is to provide **data-driven insights that can support better inventory and supply chain decisions.**

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **15,000 records** with the following attributes:

| Column             | Description                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Product_ID         | Unique identifier for each product          |
| Product_Name       | Name of the product                         |
| Category           | Product category                            |
| Supplier_Name      | Name of the supplier                        |
| Order_Date         | Date when the order was placed              |
| Delivery_Date      | Date when the order was delivered           |
| Stock_Quantity     | Available stock quantity                    |
| Reorder_Level      | Stock level at which reordering is required |
| Units_Sold         | Number of units sold                        |
| Purchase_Cost      | Cost of purchasing the product              |
| Selling_Price      | Selling price of the product                |
| Warehouse_Location | Location of the warehouse                   |
| Shipping_Time_Days | Shipping time in days                       |

## 💡 Business Insights

The project investigates several important business questions:

### 1. Which products are frequently out of stock?

Products with stock levels below their reorder levels were analyzed to identify potential inventory shortages.

### 2. Which suppliers have the highest delivery delays?

Supplier-wise shipping and delivery data were analyzed to identify suppliers with longer delivery times.

### 3. Which products generate the highest profit?

Product-level revenue, purchase cost, and selling price were analyzed to identify products contributing the most profit.

### 4. How can inventory be optimized?

Inventory levels were compared with reorder levels and sales demand to identify products that may require restocking or better inventory control.

### 5. What is the impact of delivery delays on sales?

Shipping and delivery performance were analyzed alongside sales data to understand whether longer delivery times are associated with lower sales performance.
